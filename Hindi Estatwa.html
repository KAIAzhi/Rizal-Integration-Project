Mga paniniwala, sinusuri

Three things people say about Rizal. Tap one.

Ang hindi lumingon sa pinanggalingan ay hindi makakarating sa paroroonan.Misattributed

This is a Filipino proverb that gets shared as Rizal’s own words. Fact-checkers have flagged the attribution as mistaken, so check a source before putting it in a paper. PressOne fact-check.

Rizal is the national hero because the Americans picked him.Contested

Historian Renato Constantino argued in 1969 that Rizal’s elevation under American rule suited colonial interests, and that Filipinos honored him without engaging his ideas. Others point out that Aguinaldo’s revolutionary government had already declared December 30, 1898 a day of national mourning for Rizal. Both facts are part of the story. Rizal Day, background.

Rizal rejected revolution, so he was no revolutionary.Historians still argue

In 1896 he reportedly told a Katipunan messenger that an uprising was premature, and from prison he wrote that he condemned it. Yet the Katipunan was founded the day after his arrest in 1892, and many who joined it had read his novels. Is a hero defined by his plans, or by what his work set in motion? That is your question to answer.

Starting points from scholars

Not answers, only lenses you can borrow or argue with.

Renato Constantino, “Veneration Without Understanding” (1969)

Argued that honoring Rizal as a national hero is not the same as understanding his ideas, and that his elevation under American rule suited colonial interests.

Reynaldo Ileto, Pasyon and Revolution (1979)

Studied how ordinary Filipinos understood revolt through the language of the pasyon, not only through the ideas of the educated elite.

Zeus Salazar on bayani

Filipino historians including Salazar have linked the word bayani to bayan, the community. If heroism belongs to a community, a hero is not only a lone individual.

Ambeth Ocampo, Rizal Without the Overcoat (1990)

A well-known effort to look at Rizal through everyday details and debates about his image rather than through monuments.
